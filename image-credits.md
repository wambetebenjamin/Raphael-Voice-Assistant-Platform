Candidates returned from Getty/iStock/Dreamstime/Magnific were **discarded** because the brief
restricts photography to Pexels and Unsplash only.

Unsplash: no Unsplash image was used — every Unsplash result returned for the accessibility
themes was an Unsplash+ **premium** asset (plus.unsplash.com), which is not free-licensed, so it
was rejected in favour of Pexels Licence images.
